Writeup Standards & Philosophy

01 / RECON FIRST

Observation Before Action

Every writeup documents the raw response headers, binary checksec protections, and packet captures before jumping to exploit hypotheses.

02 / DETERMINISTIC POC

Reproducible Exploits

Proof-of-concept scripts (Python or Bash) are provided so that challenge solutions can be verified and replayed in isolated test environments.

03 / SPOILER PROTECTION

Blur-to-Reveal Flags

All solved flags are protected in blurred containers. Readers can use writeups for guidance without having the solution spoiled immediately.
