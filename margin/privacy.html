Ethical Policy

Reader Privacy & Sovereignty

We believe reading should be a private act between an author and a reader. When you open a printed volume in the quiet of your room, no advertising exchange records how long you spent on page forty-two or sells your browsing affinity to a broker. We hold our digital publication to that identical standard.

1. Zero Third-Party Tracking

MARGIN uses no third-party behavioral cookies, tracking pixels, Facebook SDKs, Google Analytics beacons, or programmatic advertising networks. We do not fingerprint your hardware, record your mouse trajectories, or construct psychographic profiles.

2. Newsletter & Communications

If you voluntarily choose to subscribe to The Quarterly Dispatch, we store only your email address. We will never sell, rent, license, or share your contact information with any external organization. You may unsubscribe at any moment with a single click in any email footer.

3. Local Storage

This website uses browser localStorage purely to remember your preferences (such as your chosen reading font scale and newsletter status). This data never leaves your device.

4. Contact

Questions regarding our data ethics may be directed to privacy@margin-journal.org.
